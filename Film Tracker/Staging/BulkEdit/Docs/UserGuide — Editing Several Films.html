Editing several films at once

Select more than one film and choose Edit Metadata… (⌘E or ⌘I) to open Edit N Films. Its Details tab holds the fields that make sense for many films at once: Genre and MPAA rating, Queue Membership and automatic queueing, List Membership, Watched, Edited, Hall of Fame, Complete Data, Special Sorting, Rotation Threshold, Notes, and Tags. The Selected Films tab lists the films being edited. ⌥1 and ⌥2 switch between the tabs, as in the film sheet.

  • Each field opens to the value the films share. Where they differ, it opens blank, and a checkbox shows a dash. A field you don’t change is left as it is on every film.
  • A field you change is written to every film, replacing what each had. A blue arrow button appears at the end of its row; click it to leave that field unchanged again. Choosing the films’ shared value again also leaves it unchanged.
  • Clicking a checkbox with a dash checks it, then unchecks it, then returns it to the dash (unchanged).
  • Tags are the exception: the tags you enter are added to each film’s own, skipping any it already has.
  • List Membership sets exactly which lists the films are in: each film is taken out of any list you leave unchecked. To take the films out of every list, check a list and uncheck it again, so the field reads None.
  • If the films’ notes differ, Notes opens empty, and leaving it empty leaves every film’s notes alone. If the films share the same notes, Notes shows them, and emptying it clears them. Either way, you are asked before any film’s notes are replaced or cleared.
  • “Up Next” and the Hall of Fame have limits (8 and 200 films). If the films you selected would not fit, that choice is unavailable, and a note says how much room is left.
  • Blacklisting films sets their rotation threshold to 5 years, as it does for one film. Films that are already blacklisted keep that threshold when you change Rotation Threshold alone.
